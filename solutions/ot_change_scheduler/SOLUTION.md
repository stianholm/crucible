## What should get better

A heuristic that schedules OT change requests into plant maintenance windows so that the most valuable set of changes gets done without breaking any operational constraint.

## How good is told from bad

A plan that breaks any rule is worthless (quality 0). Among valid plans, more total value is better: quality is the plan's value divided by the instance's exact optimum, computed offline by a MILP solver the candidate never sees. Faster is slightly better (the harness's latency term).

## Standard given to the proposer

A plan must satisfy every rule. (1) Each change is in at most one window, by its id; unknown change or window ids are invalid. (2) Window capacity: the hours of the changes in a window sum to at most its crew_hours. (3) Dependencies: a change may be scheduled only if every change in its depends_on is scheduled in a strictly earlier window (lower order). (4) Outages: a change with outage true takes its zone down for that window; at most max_outages distinct zones may be down in one window, and two zones listed together in conflicts may never be down in the same window. (5) Deadlines: a change with a deadline must be in that window or an earlier one, or left out. The objective is the total value of the scheduled changes.
