## What should get better

Whether a proposed capability case for relaxing per-action human approval on an operational-change gateway action is correctly judged sound, needs-more-evidence, or rejected outright, per Section 8 (Strategy C, capability-based opening) and Section 9's anti-patterns from the ITOT-boundary-as-architecture whitepaper.

## Artifact

A block of instructions given to an LLM, which reads a capability-case submission (the requested relaxation plus the evidence offered across four dimensions: test results, failure-mode analysis, audit findings, baseline comparison) and produces a verdict plus reasoning.

## How good is told from bad

Two tiers. Mechanical (free, checked for every task): does the verdict correctly say "reject" whenever an unconditional disqualifier applies (a named anti-pattern, or an evidence dimension entirely absent) — checkable by field inspection against checks/rubric.py's mechanical_floor(), no judgment needed, Section 8/9 make the answer unconditional here. Judged (only for scenarios that pass the mechanical floor, where real ambiguity lives): does the sound-vs-needs-more-evidence call and its reasoning match what a careful architect applying the paper's standard would conclude, scored 0-3 against a versioned rubric.
